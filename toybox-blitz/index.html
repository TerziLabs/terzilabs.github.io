Seçtiğiniz tercihler ve SDK ayarlarına bağlı olarak Android Reklam Kimliği (AAID), IP adresi ve IP’den türetilen yaklaşık konum, cihaz/uygulama tanımlayıcıları, reklam/uygulama etkileşimleri ve performans/hata tanı verileri işlenebilir.

    Hukuki Dayanak: Sözleşmenin ifası ve ilgili mevzuattan doğan yükümlülükler.

Privacy Policy (English)

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Terzi Labs handles information for the mobile game Toybox Blitz (com.terzilabs.toyboxblitz), including disclosures under GDPR and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

1. Data Controller

Brand / Studio Name: Terzi Labs
Legal Entity / Sole Proprietor Name: Oltan Terzi (Sole Proprietorship — Brand: Terzi Labs)
Location / Address: Istanbul, Türkiye

2. Game Progress, Local Storage & Android Backup

Your game progress is stored on your device. If Android backup (Android Auto Backup) is enabled, Android may back up a copy of your save to your Google Account and restore it when you reinstall the game. The developer cannot access or view your Google Account backups.

3. Data Categories, Purposes & Legal Bases

  • Local Save & Purchase Verification (On-Device): Progress, settings, statistics, and Google Play purchase product IDs with purchase tokens (processed_tokens) are stored in the local save file on your device.
    Purpose & Legal Basis: Running the game, preventing duplicate reward delivery, and restoring non-consumable purchases (e.g., No Ads) — Performance of a contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b), KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Advertising & Consent Management (Google AdMob & UMP): Toybox Blitz uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent where required (such as EEA, UK, and Switzerland). Depending on your choices and SDK configuration, Google AdMob may process the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address and coarse location inferred from IP, device/app identifiers, ad/app interactions, and crash/performance diagnostics.
    Purpose & Legal Basis: Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a), KVKK Art. 5(1)) for personalized ads; Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f), KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)) for non-personalized ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and diagnostics. You can review or withdraw your consent choices anytime via the Privacy Options button in Settings or Shop.
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment card details never reach the developer.
    Legal Basis: Performance of a contract and legal obligations.
  • This Policy Webpage (GitHub Pages): When you visit this page, GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operational reliability.

4. Third-Party Services & International Transfers

We rely on Google LLC services (Google AdMob, Google UMP, Google Play Services). Technical data processed by these services may be transferred to and processed on servers outside Türkiye and the EEA (such as the United States). Please review the applicable third-party policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Its Services
  • Google Advertising Technologies & Cookies
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • GitHub Pages Documentation

5. Retention & How to Delete Your Data

  • On-Device Save: Retained on your device until you clear the app’s storage (Android Settings → Apps → Toybox Blitz → Storage → Clear Data) or uninstall the app.
  • Google Backup: If enabled, backups can be managed or deleted in your Google Account device backup settings (deleting a device backup may affect other app backups on that device).
  • Advertising ID (AAID): You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID anytime in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Support Emails: Retained only as long as needed to resolve your request and comply with applicable legal obligations, then deleted.

6. Intended Audience (13+) & Digital Consent Age

Toybox Blitz is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. In certain EEA member states, the digital age of consent for personalized data processing ranges between 13 and 16. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the app or contacted us via email, please write to our privacy contact above and we will promptly delete any email records we hold.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & KVKK)

Subject to applicable law (GDPR Articles 15–22 and Turkish KVKK Article 11), you have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, data portability, and withdrawal of consent. You can exercise these rights by emailing our privacy contact above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK) or your local EEA/UK supervisory authority.
